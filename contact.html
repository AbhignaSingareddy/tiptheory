GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, meet answers.

A few helpful details before you get in touch or plan your visit. If you need anything else, just send us a message.

Still have a question?
01 Do I need to book an appointment?

We recommend requesting an appointment in advance so your preferred service and time can be planned.

02 Can I bring a nail design reference?

Absolutely. You can describe your inspiration in the contact form and discuss the details with us before your visit.

03 How do I choose the right service?

Browse our Services page or send us a message describing your preferred look. We can help you decide what to enquire about.

04 Can I ask about press-on nails?

Yes. Select “Press-on nails” in the contact form and tell us what style or design you have in mind.

05 How do I change an appointment request?

Contact the studio with your requested date and the change you would like to discuss. We can then confirm the details.
