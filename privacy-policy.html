YOUR PRIVACY

Your Privacy Matters

TipTheory respects your privacy and aims to handle personal information responsibly. This policy explains the types of information you may provide when using our website and how that information may be used.

Please read this policy carefully before using our website or submitting information through our enquiry or appointment forms.

01. Information We Collect

When you contact TipTheory or submit information through a website form, you may choose to provide details such as:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Your email address and phone number.
  • Appointment requests, preferred dates or times.
  • The nail art or manicure services you enquire about.
  • Other information you voluntarily include in a message.

The information collected depends on the form or communication method you choose to use.

02. How We Use Your Information

Information you provide may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss requested services, manage appointment requests and communicate with you.

It may also help us maintain and improve our website and the experience it provides.

03. Appointments and Enquiries

If you submit an appointment request or enquiry, the details you provide may be used to understand your request and respond to you.

Submitting a request does not necessarily mean an appointment has been confirmed. Please refer to any confirmation you receive from TipTheory.

04. Website Usage Information

Basic technical information may be processed by the website hosting platform or services used to operate the website. Depending on the services enabled, this may include browser information, device details or website activity.

The information actually collected depends on the website configuration and the third-party services in use.

05. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies or similar technologies may be used by the website or its integrated services to support functionality or remember certain preferences, where applicable.

You can review cookie controls in your browser. Blocking certain cookies may affect how some website features work.

06. Sharing of Information

TipTheory does not need your personal information for unrelated purposes. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the website, receive enquiries, manage communications or provide a requested feature.

The handling of information by those providers is also subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

07. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

No online transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its intended purpose or applicable legal requirements.

08. Your Choices and Rights

You can choose what information you provide through our website. If you do not provide details needed to respond to a request, we may be unable to fulfil that request.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of certain personal information.

09. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. Those websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies. Please review their policies before sharing personal information with them.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorisation where required by applicable law. If you believe a child has submitted personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

11. Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our website, services or information-handling practices change. The revised version will be published on this page.

Please review this page periodically for updates.

12. Contact TipTheory

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the personal information you have provided, please contact us through our website.
